Iván Díaz

Associate Professor of Biostatistics, with tenure
Department of Population Health
NYU Grossman School of Medicine

My research lies at the intersection of causal inference, machine learning, and mathematical statistics, with applications in clinical, biomedical, and population health research. I develop methods to understand the causes of health and disease, the mechanisms through which they operate, and how their effects vary across individuals and populations.
